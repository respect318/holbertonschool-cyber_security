# CLINICAL DOWNTIME PROCEDURES: QUICK REFERENCE

## DOCUMENT 1: EPIC EHR DOWNTIME PROCEDURES
**Audience:** Registered nurses, charge nurses, attending physicians and clinical staff.

### 1. Downtime Declaration & Materials Location
* **Declaration:** Downtime is officially declared only when the primary network fails or Epic is fully unresponsive. Do not assume downtime based on a single frozen workstation. 
* **Materials:** All downtime documentation materials are physically located in the **Red Lockbox in the Central ICU Supply Room (Room 214)**. 

### 2. Medication Administration & Orders
* **Medication Documentation:** Document all administered medications immediately on the **paper medication administration record** (MAR). 
* **Signatures:** Two RN signatures are required on the paper MAR for all controlled substances and high-risk IV drips.
* **Verbal Orders:** Physicians must sign all verbal orders on the paper chart within 2 hours. The receiving RN must explicitly write "V.O. Read Back" next to the order.
* **New Orders:** All new physician orders received during downtime must be written on the carbon-copy Downtime Order Sheets located in the red lockbox.

### 3. Patient Safety & Clinical Assessments
* **Allergy Verification:** To verify patient allergies without Epic, check the patient's physical red allergy wristband AND the most recent printed patient facesheet. 
* **Escalation:** If allergies cannot be verified, use the charge nurse escalation path for high-risk medications: the Charge Nurse must contact the central pharmacy for a historical profile check before administration.
* **Assessments:** Document all clinical assessment findings, vital signs, and intake/output directly onto the Paper Clinical Flowsheet every hour.

### 4. Reconciliation & Escalation Path
* **Reconciliation:** When the system is restored, do not discard paper records. The primary RN and Attending Physician must manually reconcile paper downtime records with Epic before the end of the shift.
* **Escalation Chain:** If a critical patient care issue arises due to missing digital data, follow the strict escalation path from **charge nurse to nursing supervisor to administrator on call**.

## DOCUMENT 2: LABORATORY INFORMATION SYSTEM (LIS) DOWNTIME PROCEDURES
**Audience:** Laboratory technicians, nurses submitting specimen requests and physicians ordering labs.

### 1. Lab Orders & Prioritization
* **Prioritization:** During downtime, laboratory capacity is severely constrained. You must prioritize **STAT versus routine** orders. All routine labs are suspended; only STAT orders will be processed until the LIS is restored.
* **Submitting Orders:** To submit a STAT lab order during downtime, use the **paper requisition** forms kept in the Downtime Binder at every nursing station. 
* **Verbal Orders:** For the verbal order process, the lab technician must document the requesting physician's name, callback number, and the exact time of the call on the paper requisition.

### 2. Specimen Labeling & Tracking
* **Manual Labeling:** Without LIS-printed labels, every tube must be hand-written using a permanent marker.
* **Minimum Requirements:** You must include the **minimum required identifiers**: Patient Full Name, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number (MRN), Collection Time, and RN Initials.
* **Rejection Warning:** Missing identifiers, unreadable handwriting, or unmatched names are critical labeling errors that cause specimen rejection immediately.

### 3. Receiving Results & Critical Values
* **Receiving Results:** The lab will call the nursing unit directly with STAT results.
* **Mandatory Step:** The receiving nurse or physician must fulfill the **mandatory read-back requirement before hanging up** to confirm the exact result value. Write "Read-back confirmed" on the paper flowsheet.
* **Critical Value Reporting:** Lab technicians must report a critical value during downtime using the direct phone escalation chain: Call the Primary RN first. If no answer within 3 minutes, call the Charge Nurse. If no answer, page the Attending Physician directly.

### 4. System Restoration & Reconciliation
* **Reconciliation:** Upon LIS restoration, laboratory staff will manually reconcile paper orders and results with the LIS. Clinical staff must verify that all downtime STAT orders correctly reflect in the patient's digital chart before leaving their shift.
